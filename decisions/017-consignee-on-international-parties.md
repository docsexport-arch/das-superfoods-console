# 017 — International party form says "Consignee"; private label keeps "Ship to"

**Decided:** 2026-10-07, on the owner's instruction, with a screenshot of the
international party form. Refines 014.

014 renamed the party form's second party to "Ship to" / "Shipping address" for
every party. For an **international** party the form now says:

| Was | Is |
|---|---|
| Ship to | Consignee |
| Shipping address | Consignee address |
| Other buyer and ship-to names | Other buyer or consignee |

A **private-label / India** party is unchanged: Ship to, Shipping address,
Other buyer and ship-to names. The instruction was explicit that only the
international form changes.

It is one pair of fields under either name — the wording follows the kind of
party, the data does not. Nothing stored changes, and no document changes: the
international proforma already said "Consignee" and the private-label one
"Ship to" (decisions/014, and the removal of "Manufactured by").
