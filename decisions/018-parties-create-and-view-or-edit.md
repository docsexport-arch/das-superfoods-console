# 018 — Parties: creation on its own; the list behind "View or Edit party"

**Decided:** 2026-10-07, on the owner's instruction, with a screenshot of the
Parties page: the created parties should not sit on the party-creation screen;
a "View or Edit party" option at the top right should show all of them, under
separate International and Private label / India sections, editable and
deletable as before.

The Parties page now has two views.

**Party creation** — what the page opens on. The kind of party is picked
(International / Private label / India) and the form is open, ready to fill.
At the top right, under the toolbar, is **View or Edit party**, with the number
of parties on file beside it. After a party is created the page says so, offers
the same link, and gives a clean form for the next one. The form's second
button clears it rather than closing it — there is nothing behind it.

**View or Edit party** — every party on file, in two sections, International
first then Private label / India, each with its count. Each row has Edit and
Delete, exactly as before: Edit opens the party's form above the lists; Delete
asks first and retires the party without touching issued documents. Excel and
**Add party** (back to creation) are at the top right.

**What it rules out**

- The list is not shown on the creation screen, by design. A party is found
  again through View or Edit party, or through search (the command palette
  still finds parties by any of their names).
- Both kinds are shown together in the list view rather than behind a tab, so
  nothing on file is ever one click out of sight there.
