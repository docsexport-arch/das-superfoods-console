# 015 — Proforma header labels; no "Currency: INR" line; corrects 014

**Decided:** 2026-10-06, on the owner's instruction, with screenshots of the
printed proforma.

## Header block

The top-right block of the proforma labels everything it prints:

    PI No: …
    PI Date: …
    Buyer Order No: …
    Buyer Order Date: …

Before, the number stood bare, the date was just "Date", and the order number
and its date were run together as "Buyer order: X of dd/mm/yyyy". The two order
lines are printed only when the proforma has a buyer order number. The Excel
download uses the same four labels.

## "Currency: INR"

A rupee proforma no longer prints "· Currency: INR" on its Terms line, nor a
Currency row in Excel. The currency is still plain from "Grand total (INR)" and
the amount in words.

A proforma in **USD still states its currency**. The instruction named INR, and
an export buyer needs the currency spelled out; if it should go from those too,
that is one condition to remove.

## Correction to 014

014 read "remove: Currency: INR" as an instruction about the party form and
took the INR choice away from international parties. The owner's screenshot
shows it was about this line on the proforma. **The INR choice is back on the
party form for every party.** No party was changed in between: the restriction
only hid an option, and a party already in INR kept it.

The rest of 014 stands (Ship to / Shipping address, ports typed on the
proforma).
