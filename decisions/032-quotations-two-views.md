# 032 — Quotations: creation on one screen, every quotation made on another

**Decided:** 2026-10-10, on the owner's instruction, with a screenshot of the
Quotations page showing a created quotation listed under the form: created
quotations should not show there; add an option at the top right corner that
shows all created quotations. The same arrangement the owner asked for on
Parties (decisions/018).

## The two screens

**Quotations** — what the section opens on. The creation form, already open;
nothing else. At the top right: **View or Edit quotation**, with the number
made so far. The form's second button is **Clear form** — there is nothing
behind the form to go back to.

**View or edit quotations** — behind that button. Every quotation made, each
with **PDF · Word · Edit · Delete**, the Excel list, and **New quotation** to
return to creating one. Edit opens the quotation in the form above the list.

## After creating

The page stays on creation, the form is cleared for the next one, and a line
says what happened and where the quotation went: "Quotation DS-QUO-2026-0004
for Sample Importers Inc. was created. It is now under View or Edit
quotation." — the last words are the way there. The number shown is the one
the database gave it, typed or automatic (decisions/030).

## Two places, so Back works

The screens are `#/quotations` and `#/quotations/list` (decisions/022): the
console's Back button and the browser's arrows move between them, and a reload
stays on the one that was open. Arriving at either starts it clean — no
half-open edit, no Delete question waiting, no message from the other screen.

## Not changed

What a quotation is, how it is saved, its downloads. Proformas and shipments
keep their single page.
